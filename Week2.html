Exercise Instructions

  • Spend 5 minutes exploring the template structure (layers & frames).
  • Identify at least three design problems such as:
    • Poor visual design
    • Too much text
    • No real-world results
    • Overemphasis on process
  • Add comments directly in the Figma file.

Redesign Task

  • Create a new frame named MyPortfolio.
  • Redesign avatar, bio, and layout using shapes and text.
  • Ensure proper alignment, spacing, and hierarchy.

Reflection

Add a section titled Design Rationale and explain your design improvements in 3–4 bullet points.
