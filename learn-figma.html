Step 1: Create Your Account

Step 1: Go to the Education Page

Open the following page in your browser:

👉 https://www.figma.com/education/

Step 2: Click "Get Verified" or "Apply Now"

On the page, you will see a button to apply for the Figma Education plan.

Step 3: Sign Up or Log In

You can create an account using:

  • Your university email address
  • Google login
  • Microsoft login
  • Apple login

For academics and students, it is best to use your university email (e.g., @university.ac.uk or .edu) to speed up verification.

Step 4: Apply for Education Access

You will need to provide the following information:

  • Your name
  • University or institution
  • Academic role (student / educator)
  • Institution email address
  • Proof of affiliation (student ID, staff page, etc.)

Step 5: Wait for Verification

Figma will verify your academic status after submitting the application. This process may take a short time depending on verification checks.
